About & Methodology

Technical architecture, 14-feature contract specifications, derived calculations, and explanation methodology.

What this system estimates

The application is a personal financial digital twin for predicting next-month total outgoing debit spending (Czech bank dataset accounts). It accepts primary financial inputs, computes derived features, and generates CatBoost estimates.

Model and predictor contract

Predictions use a trained CatBoost regressor over a fixed 14-feature vector. Primary inputs are entered in Twin; derived features (current month spending, 3-month mean, 3-month population standard deviation with ddof=0) are computed automatically by the backend.

Scenarios and explanations

What-If scenarios evaluate model sensitivity by modifying one primary input, re-deriving features, and comparing baseline vs scenario estimates. TreeSHAP attributions quantify individual feature contributions to a prediction (non-causal interpretation).
